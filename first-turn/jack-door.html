The Loft After Rain

Door left open. Come in if you like.

Obsidian stone

Cool and dark on the table. Palm-sized.

Window

Rain still on the glass. City quiet beyond.

Note on the chair

Folded once. Left on purpose.

Elian —

One year. The loft’s been here the whole time, even when it was only a sketch.
The stone warms if you hold it. The rain doesn’t mind company.
Happy first turn, compass boy.
